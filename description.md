*Round 1 Judging Criteria — Total: 100 Points*

*1. Understanding of the Problem Statement — 25 Points*

Have you understood what the problem statement actually asks? Clearly explain who experiences the problem, how it affects them and which specific part your team intends to solve.

*2. Proposed Solution — 25 Points*

Does your proposed solution directly address the identified problem? The main workflow should be clear and specific enough for the judges to understand how someone would use the product.

*3. Technical Plan — 20 Points*

Explain why you selected your proposed technology stack. Identify the most technically challenging part of the solution and show how your planned architecture will address it.

*4. Originality of Approach — 15 Points*

Every team has received the same brief. Explain what makes your interpretation, solution, workflow or approach different from those of other teams.

*5. Feasibility and Scope — 15 Points*

Can your team realistically build the proposed solution within the available time? A focused and achievable solution will score higher than an unnecessarily large idea that cannot be completed.
